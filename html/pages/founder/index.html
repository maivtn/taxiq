Brian Nguyen

Founder & CEO, VLINKPAY · Founder, NEXORA TOUCH

Houston, TX

25+ years of nail industry expertise.

Brian Nguyen brings more than 25 years in the nail industry and more than 27 years in network marketing. He has advised major companies in technology, finance and fintech, and has extensive experience in blockchain and AI. He still operates BITCOIN NAIL BAR in Houston.

“You take care of your clients. We take care of everything else.”

Why I'm building this

America's nail industry was built by the small hands of Vietnamese people. Vietnamese refugees came to America with empty hands — and from those hands, one manicure at a time, one small salon at a time, they built a multi-billion-dollar industry across the United States. The nail industry gave the Vietnamese a chance: a trade to earn a living, a shop to call their own, a future for their children in America. I have spent more than 25 years in this industry, and I owe it my livelihood. That is why I built VLINKPAY — not just to sell software, but to preserve this industry and raise it to a new level with technology, so the next generation of Vietnamese inherits a nail industry worth being proud of — the way it gave my generation its opportunity.

Vision, mission and company principles

Working language supplied by Brian Nguyen. Pending CEO approval.

01VISION

What VLINKPAY aims to become

VLINKPAY aims to become a trillion-scale technology and fintech group — a Vietnamese brand born in America, reaching the world and becoming a source of pride for the Vietnamese community. We envision a future in which small business owners, beginning with the people who built the nail industry, can access technology, resources and opportunities once reserved for large enterprises. With less weight from daily operations, they can spend more time caring for customers, create lasting jobs and open a better path for the next generation. VLINKPAY's growth will matter most when the businesses growing alongside us can rise, too.

02MISSION

What VLINKPAY is building

VLINKPAY builds an ecosystem of AI and fintech platforms — payments, POS, marketplace, crypto, tax and travel — starting with the Vietnamese business community in the U.S. and expanding to small businesses worldwide. Each platform handles everything around its customers' craft ('Owners focus on their customers. We handle everything else'), growing brand by brand into the pillars of the group — on the path to a public listing around 2030 and to trillion scale.

03CORE VALUES

What we stand for

  • Rooted in the craft. Build from real salon operations and test at BITCOIN NAIL BAR before selling.
  • Data over guesswork. Base decisions on visible numbers, not luck.
  • Candor and integrity. Never invent numbers or overpromise. Own mistakes promptly and correct them openly.
  • Our partners own their customers. The platform never competes with salons for their customers.
  • Vietnamese ingenuity, global standards. Build for the Vietnamese community first, to a standard that can compete worldwide.
  • Lift the community together. Support it through VLINKPAY FOUNDATION and AI Business Mastery 90 Days training.
04CULTURE CREED

How we work

  • Speak directly and tell the truth from the start.
  • If it has not been verified, it is not done. Never call work finished without seeing the real result.
  • Sell customers only what the team has actually used in its own operations.
  • Respect decisions once made. If one changes, explain what changed and why; never revise it quietly.
  • Never invent numbers, in any form.
  • Own mistakes the same day, fix the root cause and do not shift blame.
  • The ultimate measure: more customers for salons, better income for technicians and less burden on owners.
