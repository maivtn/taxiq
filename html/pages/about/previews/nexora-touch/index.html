OUR STORY

Why NEXORA TOUCH exists

A better day for the people behind every appointment.

Built from the daily pressures of a working salon in Houston, NEXORA TOUCH asks a simple question: what if technology handled more of the work around the service?

01 / THE MOMENT

Care happens in front of the guest. The rest of the day still needs attention.

A guest arrives as the phone rings. A technician needs the next appointment. Later, the owner must make sense of services, payments and follow-up. Inside BITCOIN NAIL BAR, the team saw how much of this work competed for the same attention.

That experience became the starting point for NEXORA TOUCH: tools designed around the rhythm of a salon, with more room for people to focus on their guests.
