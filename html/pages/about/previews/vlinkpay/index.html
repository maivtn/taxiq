OUR STORY

Why VLINKPAY began

For Vietnamese families in America, the nail industry became more than a trade. It became a way to earn a living, open a shop and build a future for their children. Brian Nguyen spent more than 25 years in that world. He saw the skill and care inside the salon, and the heavy work owners carried behind it.

VLINK was established on January 10, 2019. The path that followed had its turns and setbacks, but the same practical problem kept coming into focus: payments, staff, guest relationships and business decisions were spread across tools that did not reflect a working salon. Brian founded VLINKPAY in Houston in 2022 to build technology around that reality. New products followed as different parts of that work called for their own solutions.

THE FOUNDER

Brian Nguyen

Founder & CEO, VLINKPAY · Houston, Texas

Built from the salon floor.

After more than 25 years in the U.S. nail industry, Brian still works close to its daily realities through BITCOIN NAIL BAR in Houston. NEXORA TOUCH grew from the problems owners and staff face at a real front desk.

He founded VLINKPAY in Houston in 2022, following VLINK's establishment in 2019. He has advised major organizations in technology, finance and fintech and brings experience in blockchain and AI.

“That is why I built VLINKPAY — not just to sell software, but to preserve this industry and raise it to a new level with technology.”
