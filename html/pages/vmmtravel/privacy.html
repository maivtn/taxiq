Information you provide

The planning inquiry form lets you enter a program of interest, preferred time, group size, name, email, optional phone number, and optional notes. Its button prepares an email in your mail app; it does not send information to VMMTRAVEL until you choose Send. Any email you send is handled by your mail provider and its recipient.

Third-party content

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and video preview images from YouTube. Playing a video or opening an external link connects your browser to that service. It may receive your IP address and device data and use cookies under its own policy.

Technical data on a hosting service

The hosting provider may keep basic access logs to operate and protect this site. Retention periods and data processors must be confirmed in the official version before publication.

Choices and contact

You can choose not to play videos or open external links, and you can manage cookies in your browser. For privacy questions, use the VMM ecosystem email in the contact section. This policy must be updated if direct form submission, bookings, analytics, or payments are added.
