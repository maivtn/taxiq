📥 Check-in from the Nexora App / QR — waiting on the front desk (0)

📐 SPEC — Check-in request: Three doors feed the same queue: (1) the Nexora App — a known guest, identified by their account; (2) a QR that opens the web check-in with no app install — new guests; (3) the kiosk or the front desk. App and QR create a request — the front desk taps ✓ to accept (into the queue, keeping the requested tech and any promo/reward already chosen) or ✕ to decline (the app tells the guest why: fully booked, tech off…). Once confirmed, the guest's app switches to the Live Ticket.

🔐 Tech access requests — waiting on front desk / manager (0)

📐 SPEC — Access request: A tech scans the QR on a guest's ticket in the Staff App. If the ticket is already theirs it opens straight to the work ticket. Someone else's ticket — or an unassigned one — is blocked, with a "Request access" button that lands here together with a skill check. Approving assigns or reassigns the tech and writes it to the ticket's audit log; if they lack the skill the override must be justified (production requires a manager PIN). Declining reports back to the Staff App. This is what stops techs from jumping tickets to grab guests.

📅 Today's bookings & ETA — guests on the way (0)

📐 SPEC — Booking & ETA (#79): Solves "the guest has an appointment but nobody knows when they'll show". The guest taps 🚗 On my way in the app and the salon sees a live ETA here, in lanes: Booked → On the way → Arrived → Checked in. Privacy has two levels: by default only the ETA is shared; the guest can opt into 📍 Share live trip for an Uber-style view. Live trip location only runs in the foreground while moving (no permanent background permission — easier App Store review, kinder to the battery), updates every 30–60s over websocket, and shuts off on the arrival geofence or after 60 minutes. Guests without the app get an SMS at T-60 with a web link that does the same thing. Production adds a geofence that detects arrival automatically plus AI prep that holds the requested tech and readies the chair.

⏳ Queue

💅 Techs on shift — turns today

📐 SPEC S2 — Dispatch (floor): Guests enter the queue from the kiosk, the front desk by hand, or an online booking (auto-surfaced 15 minutes before the slot). Statuses run waiting → in-service → ready-to-pay → paid — when the tech taps ✋ Done the guest moves to "ready to pay" and the tech is freed to take the next guest immediately (waiting to pay over 5 minutes turns the chip red). Two-tap assign: tap the guest card and the free techs become assign buttons. ▶ is the one-tap version: the requested tech if they are free, otherwise the tech with the fewest turns (fair rotation, marked 🎯 Next up). In-service cards get a ⇄ to swap techs without counting an extra turn. 🧰 Skill match: selecting a guest scores every tech (the service's required skill weighs heaviest, plus turn fairness and an explicit request); assigning a tech who lacks the skill is blocked with a warning and needs a second tap to override, which is written to the ticket's audit log (production requires a manager PIN). Skills are configured in Management. Wait timers run on each card and a wait over 20 minutes turns the card red. The board refreshes every 30 seconds and syncs across devices over websocket.

📐 SPEC S14 — QR time clock: The POS shows a QR carrying a token that rotates every 15 seconds and is single-use. The server verifies TTL + single-use, so the timestamp cannot be forged from the POS. On clock-in the system orders turns by clock-in time (the owner can switch the rule to appointment-based or rotation) and pushes a notification (FCM/APNs) with the clock-in time, turn position, and Station #. Each tech owns a fixed Station # — editable right in the table. A missed clock-out auto-closes and is flagged for owner approval. 📟 Beeper numbers are handed out in arrival order and reset at midnight.

📐 SPEC S21 — SMS & Marketing (#94): In production this is not a separate app — the SMS engine is a module on the same backend sharing the POS customer database, so segments are computed live off the ledger instead of being entered twice. POS events (bill closed, rebook, refund, gift card, booking) fire webhooks that trigger journeys. Access is limited to owner/manager; front desk and techs never see this tab. Sending is gated on TCPA consent — a guest who never opted in is blocked automatically, and every message carries the STOP footer.

📅 Appointments

🟪 tech assigned · 🟧 unassigned
📐 SPEC S12 — Staff appointments: Same bookings table as the public web booking (S11) and the kiosk (S3) — realtime sync. Columns are per tech; unassigned bookings get their own orange column for the front desk to assign later. Production reschedules by drag-and-drop; this prototype uses the form. Double-booking is checked server-side on tech+time (the client only warns). SMS confirmation on create/edit/cancel plus a reminder 2 hours ahead. A no-show past 30 minutes auto-cancels and returns the slot to the pool. Front desk and up can edit; techs only see their own column.
