Privacy Policy

Effective Date: Jun 26, 2026
Last Updated: Jul 18, 2026

1. Introduction

NEXORA TOUCH LLC ("Nexora", "we", "us") operates the NEXORA TOUCH platform — a technology service for service businesses that includes digital tipping and reviews, an AI phone receptionist that answers business phone lines and books appointments, and related SMS text notifications. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you use the Service. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide: account information (name, email, phone number), business information (for merchants), and content you upload (profile photos, business logos).

Caller and booking information: when a customer calls a business phone line answered by our AI receptionist service, we collect the caller's phone number and, when provided by the caller, their name, requested service, and preferred appointment time, in order to deliver the service — including booking the appointment and sending SMS confirmations and follow-ups. Calls may be recorded for quality and training purposes, and callers are handled in accordance with applicable law. When a customer submits an online booking request, we collect the information they enter (such as name, phone number, and requested service).

Automatically collected information: usage data, IP address, device and browser information, and session activity collected via the platform and Google Analytics.

Guest tipping interactions: when customers use the tipping flow as guests, we record anonymous session data (touch points visited, tip amounts, ratings) without requiring the guest's name or payment details.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Operate and improve the Service
  • Answer business phone calls and book appointments through the AI receptionist
  • Send SMS text messages such as appointment confirmations, missed-call follow-ups, customer care responses, review requests, and — with consent — occasional promotional offers
  • Manage accounts and communicate with users (email and push notifications)
  • Detect fraud and enforce our Terms of Service
  • Analyze platform usage and performance
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. SMS and Mobile Information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Customers opt in to receive SMS messages by verbally requesting an appointment or follow-up during a phone call to a participating business's phone number, or by submitting an online booking request with their phone number. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Customers can opt out at any time by replying STOP, and can get help by replying HELP or contacting support@nexoratouch.com or (832) 979-5559.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate the platform — including telecommunications and SMS delivery providers, analytics, email delivery, push notifications, and cloud infrastructure. These providers are contractually bound to use your information only as necessary to perform services on our behalf. We may also disclose information to comply with legal obligations or to protect the rights and safety of users. Consistent with Section 4, mobile numbers and SMS opt-in data are never shared for third-party marketing.

6. Payment Information

Nexora does not collect, store, or process any payment information. Tips are sent directly between customers and staff through third-party payment applications. We have no access to financial information, bank account numbers, or card details.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide the Service or as required by law. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting support@nexoratouch.com.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing.

  • EU/EEA users (GDPR): access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction, and objection
  • California residents (CCPA): rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale (we do not sell data)

To exercise your rights, contact support@nexoratouch.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies (required for authentication and session management) and analytics cookies (Google Analytics). You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on or adjusting your browser settings.

10. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact support@nexoratouch.com and we will delete it promptly.

11. Security

We implement reasonable technical and administrative safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted data transmission and secure cloud infrastructure. No transmission method is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated by email or in-app notice. Continued use of the Service constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
