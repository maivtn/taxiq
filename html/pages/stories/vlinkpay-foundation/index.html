VLINKPAY FOUNDATION

An opportunity received. A path opened for others.

For many Vietnamese families in America, the nail industry offered a trade, a livelihood and a chance to build a future. Brian Nguyen knows the weight of that opportunity after more than 25 years in the industry. Out of that gratitude, VLINKPAY FOUNDATION looks toward homes, scholarships and a charitable fund for people facing hardship.

Follow the story ↓
01 The story

Let the opportunity that lifted one generation reach the next.

Behind every small salon is more than a business. There are hands that learned a craft, families that held on through difficult years, and children growing up with possibilities their parents worked to create. The U.S. nail industry gave many Vietnamese families that first foothold; Brian Nguyen's own story is tied to it.

For the foundation, gratitude needs to become something people can feel in daily life: the security of a home, the chance to stay in school, and help when a family is struggling. These are the directions it hopes to build toward, carrying the opportunity that once supported one generation to the next.

02 WHAT WE HOPE TO BUILD

Care should have a shape people can see.

The foundation's charitable direction begins with three practical hopes. They are goals for the future, not a claim that these programs already operate.

01

A place to call home

Work toward support for building or securing safe homes for families in need. A stable place to live can give a family room to begin again.

02

A chance to keep learning

Work toward providing scholarships for students who need help continuing their education. Opportunity should not end because a family cannot carry every cost alone.

03

Help in hard moments

Work toward a charitable fund that can support people living in poverty or facing hardship, with care directed to real needs.

As this work takes shape, the foundation can listen to the community and identify other meaningful ways to help. No grant amounts, eligibility rules or start dates have been announced.

04 The bigger picture

Part of the VLINKPAY ecosystem

VLINKPAY's platforms serve small businesses. VLINKPAY FOUNDATION gives the group's broader value a charitable direction: work toward homes, scholarships and help in hard times, so success can strengthen the community that helped create it.

Homes, scholarships and a charitable fund are goals for the foundation; specific programs, funding and launch dates have not been announced.
