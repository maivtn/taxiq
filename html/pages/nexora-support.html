Frequently Asked Questions

How to generate QR codes?

Go to the Stations tab, click "+ Add New Station", enter the station name, and assign a staff member if needed. The QR code is generated instantly and can be printed or simulated.

How do direct tips work?

Nexora stations send tips directly to the technician's connected Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, or VLINKPAY address. Funds go straight to their account without processing delays or owner intervention.

How to set up VLINKPAY Wallet?

Navigate to Settings -> Profile tab, scroll down to Receive Payment Methods, and enter your VLINKPAY Wallet identifier. Once verified, customers will see VLINKPAY as a direct tipping option.

How to order NFC stands?

You can order custom laser-engraved NFC wooden stands or tabletop cards directly from the Manage Plan or Subscriptions tab, or by contacting our support team with your salon station count.

How do my customers tip without an app?

Customers scan the station QR code with their phone camera — no app download required. They choose a tip amount, select their preferred payment method (Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, or VLINKPAY), and complete the payment in their existing wallet app.

When do tips arrive in my technician's account?

Tips go directly from the customer to the technician's connected payout account. There is no hold or batching by Nexora — funds appear as soon as the customer's payment app confirms the transfer, typically within minutes.

Can I use Nexora Touch with my existing POS system?

Yes. Nexora Touch runs alongside your existing POS — it handles tipping and reviews at each station without replacing your checkout system. Your POS continues to process service payments; Nexora captures tips and feedback via QR codes at each workstation.

What happens if a customer leaves a 1-star review?

Low ratings are routed to you privately first so you can respond before they appear publicly. You receive a notification with the review details and can address the concern directly with the customer or your team.

How do I add or remove a technician from my roster?

Go to Staff in your dashboard. To add a technician, click Invite Staff and share the join link or QR code. To remove someone, open their profile and deactivate or remove them from your roster — their tip history remains in your records.

Is there a contract or can I cancel anytime?

Nexora Touch has no long-term contract. You can cancel your subscription at any time from Manage Plan. Your account stays active through the end of your current billing period.
